## 📂 Available Plugins in this Repository (7 Integrations)

We have categorized our plugins to help you find exactly what you need to grow your business:

### 🏢 1. Odoo ERP WhatsApp Modules (v15, v16, v17, v18)
Fully functional Odoo apps ready to be installed in your ERP environment.
*   **`/fiveminutesapi_invoice`**: Automate PDF Invoice delivery & ZATCA alerts to customers via WhatsApp.
*   **`/fiveminutesapi_pos`**: Send paperless Digital Receipts directly from Odoo Point of Sale (POS) to WhatsApp.
*   **`/fiveminutesapi_otp`**: 6-Digit OTP verification module for highly secure Odoo logins and customer validations.

### 🛒 2. E-Commerce Stores (WooCommerce, Shopify)
*   **`/woocommerce-cod`**: The ultimate Fake COD Blocker. Sends an instant WhatsApp OTP verification to customers. When they click `[ ✅ Confirm ]` on WhatsApp, the order status in WooCommerce changes to "Processing" automatically.

### 📊 3. Spreadsheets & CRMs (Google Sheets)
*   **`/google-sheets-crm`**: A powerful 50-line Google Apps Script that turns your Google Sheet into a 2-Way automated CRM. Perfect for Clinics, Real Estate, and Schools. When a customer replies to your reminder via WhatsApp, the row in your sheet turns Green!

### 🧾 4. Web Hosting & Billing (WHMCS)
*   **`/whmcs-billing`**: Action hook script for WHMCS. Automatically sends Meta-approved WhatsApp Templates for new invoices, payment reminders, and account expiry alerts to your hosting clients.

### 💻 5. Developer Tools (Node.js & Supabase)
*   **`/nodejs-supabase-auth`**: Drop-in Authentication Hooks. Replace expensive SMS Twilio verifications with ultra-fast, highly secure WhatsApp OTPs for your Next.js and Flutter apps.
